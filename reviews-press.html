SP
Sarah PhuLocal Guide · 216 reviews · 1,096 photos
Google review
Pho Ngoc Yen II is probably my favourite Vietnamese restaurant in Etobicoke. The space has a welcoming atmosphere, decorated with plenty of greenery, and has a large patio that’s perfect during warmer months. There is plenty of free parking on site, although the restaurant can get quite busy. On one occasion, the lot was completely full and I had to wait a bit for a spot, which speaks to its popularity. The staff are friendly and attentive, and payment is made at the front counter after your meal. One thing that sets this restaurant apart is its extensive menu. While the pho is good, there are also many other Vietnamese and Thai dishes that you don’t typically find at most Vietnamese restaurants, making it a great place to explore beyond the usual Vietnamese classics.
3 months agoDinner · CA$30–40
AP
Artist PierreLocal Guide · 17 reviews · 26 photos
Google review
Our group included one Vietnamese diner, one Briton, and one Canadian, so we had a mix of perspectives around the table. We waited about 15 minutes for a table, which wasn’t bad considering how busy it was. Once we were seated, the service was outstanding. Every staff member we interacted with was friendly, attentive, and knowledgeable. Whether we had questions about the menu, wanted to add another dish, or needed advice on the wine selection, someone was always happy to help. We never felt rushed or like we were overstaying our welcome. As someone relatively new to Vietnamese cuisine, I appreciated how welcoming the experience was. The food was fresh, beautifully prepared, and packed with flavor. While it’s a little on the pricey side, the quality of the ingredients and the care that went into each dish made it worthwhile. With one Vietnamese guest at our table, it was reassuring to hear that the food met their expectations too. By the end of the meal, all three of us agreed that it had been an excellent dining experience. The combination of genuine hospitality and delicious food left such a positive impression that we happily left a generous tip. We’ll definitely be back.
