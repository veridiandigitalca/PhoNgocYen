The best pho I ever had! What a taste and what an awesome experience dining here! The place was crowded and it took about 30 mins or so for us to get a table. Considering we were 9 people in our party, wasn’t a bad wait at all. The service is absolutely exemplary. The manager and staff are very kind and serve with a smile. There were good amount of vegan options for me. My only ask to the management would be to consider adding at least one vegan dessert in the menu. Highly recommend this place and you will love the food and the service! Food: 5/5 · Service: 5/5 · Atmosphere: 5/5 · Group size: 9+ people.

Honestly, not much to say about this place aside from it probably being the best Vietnamese restaurant in the GTA. The food is incredible and speaks for itself. Absolute must tries are the pho and fish sauce wings. Some of the best wings and the best pho broth I’ve ever had. Service is friendly, accommodating, and fast. The atmosphere is lively and the decor is fun and inviting. Prices are reasonable and quality is exceptional. Definitely give it a try and I can almost guarantee it won’t be your last trip here. Highly, highly recommend!

SP
Sarah PhuLocal Guide · 216 reviews · 1,096 photos
Google review
Pho Ngoc Yen II is probably my favourite Vietnamese restaurant in Etobicoke. The space has a welcoming atmosphere, decorated with plenty of greenery, and has a large patio that’s perfect during warmer months. There is plenty of free parking on site, although the restaurant can get quite busy. On one occasion, the lot was completely full and I had to wait a bit for a spot, which speaks to its popularity. The staff are friendly and attentive, and payment is made at the front counter after your meal. One thing that sets this restaurant apart is its extensive menu. While the pho is good, there are also many other Vietnamese and Thai dishes that you don’t typically find at most Vietnamese restaurants, making it a great place to explore beyond the usual Vietnamese classics.
